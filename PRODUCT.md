# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Inferred from the current app: people who want a focused phone session and enjoy earning a companion collection as they build the habit. The primary workflow is mobile.

## Product Purpose

Inferred from the current app: Focus Tracker runs strict focus sessions. A completed session adds progress and can earn coins, streak rewards, and new pets.

## Positioning

The pet collection is the progression system: pets unlock through focused minutes, completed sessions, and streak milestones.

## Operating Context

The app is built with Next.js and Capacitor for iOS and Android. During an active session, touching the screen, leaving or reloading the app, or detected device movement interrupts the attempt. Users can choose preset or custom durations and resume with a new attempt after an interruption.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing timer, interruption, reward, streak, pet unlock, shop, and theme behavior during visual changes.
- The current app supports focus coins, daily goal and gift claims, streak check-ins, streak protection and repair, 22 unlockable pets, accessories, themes, reminders, achievements, and session history.
- Progress is stored on the device. The current product has no account, ads, or tracking.
- The interface is a shared React UI packaged for iOS and Android with Capacitor.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Focus Tracker.
- Keep the blue and white palette as the base identity.
- Use friendly illustrated pet mascots across the 22-species collection.
- Give the interface a refined, native-feeling treatment while preserving the current product flows.

## Evidence on Hand

The current product implementation, copy, game-state model, and asset catalog are in this repository. There are no customer quotes, commercial claims, or external proof assets to add.

## Product Principles

- Make the next focus action easy to understand.
- Keep the rules and status of rewards clear.
- Let focused time unlock the companion collection.
- Keep progress on the user's device.
- Preserve the existing product behavior when improving presentation.
